# Mechanical Architecture

Airaavat-V1 is a physically fabricated UGV platform.

The mechanical development process emphasizes iterative prototyping: dimensions, reinforcement and structural arrangements are revised based on observed behaviour during assembly and ground trials.

## Major mechanical subsystems

### Chassis
Fabricated manually using cutting/grinding, drilling, fastening and reinforcement.

### Drivetrain
The V1 drivetrain uses a brushed-DC drive motor with a jackshaft and sprocket-chain reduction.

### Steering
The vehicle uses an Ackermann steering arrangement actuated by a high-torque servo.
